# Cross-modal alignment metrics

Phase 3A · 2026-09-12 · proposed design; no local performance results.

For paired observations, retrieval evaluates whether z_i^RNA retrieves z_i^ADT/ATAC. Record recall@k, median rank and FOSCTTM with exact candidate pool, direction and tie handling. A paired ID supplies an observation correspondence; it does not imply all modalities encode identical biology. Imputed targets are not independent measurements.
