# Scope and inclusion

Phase 3A · 2026-09-12 · proposed design; no local performance results.

The frozen scope is tabular spatial multi-omics representation and integration with biological structure preservation central. Suites are L (human lymph-node A1/D1: RNA+ADT+spatial) and B (mouse embryonic brain E11/E13/E15/E18: RNA+ATAC+spatial where available). Candidate inclusion requires task/modality/observation compatibility, tabular support, recoverable implementation, adequate description, reproducible dependencies, feasible scale and a meaningful comparison. This is a design record; no method is fitted.
