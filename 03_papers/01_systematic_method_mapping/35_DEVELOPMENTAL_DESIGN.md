# Developmental design

Phase 3A · 2026-09-12 · proposed design; no local performance results.

Within-stage performance asks whether a method preserves structure within E11, E13, E15 or E18. Across-stage consistency asks transfer or comparability across stages. Do not merge stage labels as nuisance batches without a biological question. Stage-specific peak harmonization and embryo/replicate identity are prerequisites.
