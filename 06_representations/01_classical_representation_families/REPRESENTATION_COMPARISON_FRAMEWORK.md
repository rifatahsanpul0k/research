# Representation comparison framework (unranked)

This is a structured review schema, not a scorecard. “studied_not_fitted” means the family and its assumptions were reviewed; no project instance, benchmark or winner is implied. Spatial/multimodal/missing support describes the mathematical family or representative method and must be checked for the exact implementation later.
