# Phase 1B sources and evidence limits

Access date: 2026-09-11. Only sources used in these notes are listed. Bibliographic verification establishes publication identity; it is not full-method review or experimental reproduction. References are separated by source class in papers.csv. The cited ENCODE 2020 article is a historical publication, not a statement of current registry size or release.

All numerical examples are synthetic. Topic footnotes give supporting locations; source records preserve access depth and phase contribution. Some direct publisher/PMC requests returned subscription previews, cookie errors or browser challenges; authoritative bibliographic records and indexed article passages were used where documented. No restricted body or supplement is claimed as inspected.

## Scope of source use

Reviews synthesize biological mechanisms; primary studies supply focused evidence for nucleosome structure, accessibility measurement, silencer function, enhancer marks, regulatory perturbation and modified-base ambiguity. No computational performance result, disease association or dataset-specific developmental conclusion is extracted. Scientific conclusions never use Graphify output.

Both works already existed correctly identified in papers.csv; no duplicate rows were added.
