# Sources used in Phase 1C

Access date: 2026-09-11. Focused biological reading, not an exhaustive review. Every entry below is cited in a completed topic or integration note. Full bibliographic rows are in [papers.csv](../../papers.csv). The 14 references added during preparation are retained; 19 additional sources were used during completion. Earlier registered references are reused without duplicate rows.

Access depth is stated per source. Indexed passages and abstracts support only the extracted claims; a PMC challenge or subscription preview is not called a full-text read. No supplements, protocols, code, training results or datasets were downloaded/reproduced. Primary mouse studies illustrate mechanisms and evidence types; they do not validate the human LN or embryonic-brain project annotations. Institutional resources have unknown dates where no publication date was supplied.
