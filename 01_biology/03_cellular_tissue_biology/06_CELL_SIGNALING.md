# Cell signaling

Phase 1C; biological foundations. Numerical examples are synthetic unless explicitly identified as user-provided metadata.

## A. Biological meaning

A ligand is a molecule that binds a receptor; a receptor couples recognition to a cellular response. Intracellular signaling changes molecular activity, sometimes followed by altered transcription. Signals include more than proteins, and some receptors act inside cells.[^SIGNAL]

## B. Mechanism

Cell A produces or presents a ligand → it reaches a competent receptor of cell B → intracellular activity changes → regulatory or protein-state responses follow. Cascades may involve phosphorylation and small second messengers such as calcium or cyclic AMP.[^SIGNAL][^PROT]

| Mode | Relationship |
|---|---|
| Autocrine | Producing cell can respond to its own signal |
| Paracrine | Local extracellular signaling |
| Endocrine | Circulation carries a signal to distant targets |
| Contact-dependent | Surface-associated signaling requires contact |

These modes describe delivery, not guaranteed activation.[^SIGNAL]

## C. Relationship to prior concepts

Proteins from Phase 1A participate in signaling; Phase 1B regulation determines response competence. A stimulus may change existing protein activity before a new RNA pattern becomes measurable.[^PROT] Signaling can influence cycle and differentiation without redefining a cell's broad type.

## D. Experimental observation/measurement

Observing ligand/receptor proteins, receptor engagement and downstream biochemical changes addresses different steps. A functional perturbation with appropriate controls provides different evidence from co-expression. Transcriptional response alone can be consistent with several upstream causes.[^SIGNAL][^PROT] No experiment is designed or executed here.

## E. Computational representation

Synthetic evidence record:

| sender | receiver | ligand | receptor | expression_support | downstream_response | causal_support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | B | L1 | R1 | RNA_detected | unknown | unknown |

L1 and R1 are invented identifiers. A directional table, evidence-labeled network, pathway membership set or time-indexed response tensor are possible forms; none is automatically a verified communication event.

## F. Relevance to our project

For lymph-node contacts and brain patterning, expression compatibility is a candidate mechanism. Spatial information can constrain delivery possibilities, but exact reach depends on signal mode and tissue context. Future biological similarity must not substitute for sender–receiver evidence.

## G. Common misconceptions

Ligand RNA + receptor RNA does not prove active communication. Receptor abundance is not receptor activation. A signaling response need not require new protein synthesis. Answered check: matching expression leaves delivery, protein competence and causal direction unresolved.[^SIGNAL][^PROT]
